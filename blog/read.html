Read

What is stopping you from reading a few pages every day?

When in doubt, pick up a book to read. It could be anything. Make time to read a few pages every day.

It's easier than a few other hobbies, like writing, which takes time and effort. (Which is not bad at all! I would like to write about that later in another post.)

If you don't want to read to learn, read for fun.

Pick up a random book that was lying around for a long time.

Or, pick up the book you see in the bookstore on the way to work.

Or, you have your phone, don't you? Buy an e-book, or download a free e-book.

While waiting for something in a queue, read.

Or, when you are bored, read.

Make it a part of your life; a habit.

Don't worry about what others say about your hobby.

Others might judge you since you picked up a book instead of scrolling on the phone. Don't let them discourage you.

You read, which already gives you an advantage.

Read for yourself, not for others
